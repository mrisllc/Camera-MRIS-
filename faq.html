Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions

How do I take a photo? +
Open the app and tap the large shutter button at the bottom center of the screen. You can also press the volume buttons to take a photo on most devices.
How do I switch between front and rear camera? +
Tap the camera switch icon (usually at the top or bottom right corner) to toggle between the front and rear cameras.
How do I record a video? +
Swipe to Video mode or tap the video icon, then press the shutter button to start recording. Press it again to stop.
How do I apply filters? +
Tap the filter icon while in camera view. Select any filter to preview it in real time, then take the photo.
What is Night Mode? +
Night Mode improves photo quality in low-light conditions. It activates automatically in dark environments, or you can turn it on manually from the mode options.
Where are my photos saved? +
All photos and videos are saved directly to your device’s gallery. We do not upload or store your media on our servers.
Why can’t the app access my camera? +
Go to your phone Settings → Apps → Camera App → Permissions, and make sure Camera permission is allowed.
The app is crashing. What should I do? +
Try these steps: Force close the app, restart your phone, clear the app cache, or update to the latest version from the app store.
Does the app collect my personal data? +
No. Camera App is privacy-focused. We do not collect or store your photos or personal information.
How can I contact support? +
You can email us at support@cameraapp.com. We usually respond within 24–48 hours.
