Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Camera App ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

We designed Camera App with privacy in mind. Here's what we collect:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect only for the following purposes:

3. Data Storage & Security

All photos and videos you capture remain on your device. We do not have access to your media files. Any anonymous analytics data is stored securely and is not linked to your identity.

4. Third-Party Services

Camera App may use third-party services (such as analytics tools) that collect anonymous data. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Since we don’t store your photos, there is nothing to request deletion of from our side

6. Children's Privacy

Camera App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
