How to Master Night Photography

7 min read · Sep 5, 2026
Night photography can be challenging, but with the right techniques you can get amazing results. - Use Night Mode when available. - Keep your phone as steady as possible. - Look for light sources like street lamps or neon signs. - Avoid using flash in most cases. - Shoot in RAW if your phone supports it. - Use a tripod or rest your phone on a stable surface. Practice in different lighting conditions to understand how your camera handles darkness.
