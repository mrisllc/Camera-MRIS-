Help Center

Find answers to common questions about Camera App

🚀 Getting Started

How do I take a photo? +
Open the app and tap the large shutter button at the bottom center of the screen. You can also use volume buttons as a shutter on most devices.
How do I switch between front and rear camera? +
Tap the camera switch icon (usually located at the top or bottom right) to toggle between the front and rear cameras.
How do I record a video? +
Swipe to the Video mode or tap the video icon, then press the shutter button to start and stop recording.

✨ Features

How do I apply filters? +
Tap the filter icon while in camera mode. Browse available filters and tap one to apply it in real time before taking the photo.
What is Night Mode and how do I use it? +
Night Mode automatically activates in low-light conditions, or you can enable it manually from the mode options. Hold the phone steady for best results.
Can I use the grid for better composition? +
Yes. Go to Settings → Camera → Grid and turn it on. A rule-of-thirds grid will appear on the viewfinder.

🔧 Troubleshooting

The app crashes when I open it. What should I do? +
Try these steps: 1) Force close the app and reopen it. 2) Restart your device. 3) Clear the app cache from your phone settings. 4) Update the app if a new version is available.
Photos are blurry. How can I fix this? +
Make sure the lens is clean. Hold the phone steady and ensure there is enough light. Try tapping on the subject to focus before taking the photo.
Why can’t the app access my camera? +
Go to your device Settings → Apps → Camera App → Permissions and make sure Camera and Microphone permissions are allowed.

🔒 Privacy & Storage

Where are my photos saved? +
All photos and videos are saved directly to your device’s gallery or camera folder. We do not upload or store your media on our servers.
Does the app collect my personal data? +
No. Camera App is privacy-focused. We do not collect or store your photos or personal information. You can read our full Privacy Policy for more details.
